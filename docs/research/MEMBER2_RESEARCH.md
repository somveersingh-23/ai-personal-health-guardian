# Member 2 Research Basis and Engineering Decisions

Research checked on 2026-08-29. Prefer the linked primary/authoritative material over thresholds or claims generated by an AI assistant.

## Android Health Connect

- [Health Connect overview](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect): consented on-device health/fitness datastore and current SDK guidance.
- [Data types and permissions](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/data-types): record shapes, feature flags, per-type permissions, background-read and history permissions.
- [Synchronize data](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/sync-data): separate changes tokens, upsert/delete processing, source IDs, token expiry and recovery.
- [Permissions UX](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/ui/permissions): pause sync, manage access, insufficient-access and repeated-cancellation states.
- [Metadata requirements](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/metadata): preserve recording method and device type when records are produced.
- [Availability](https://developer.android.com/health-and-fitness/health-connect/availability): Android/API/device limitations, including unsupported work profiles.
- [Health Connect Jetpack releases](https://developer.android.com/jetpack/androidx/releases/health-connect): stable `connect-client:1.1.0` is used; alpha APIs are not required by the production path.
- [Android Gradle plugin 9.3 compatibility](https://developer.android.com/build/releases/agp-9-3-0-release-notes): compile SDK 37, Gradle 9.5 and JDK 17 toolchain basis.

Decision: implement capability-driven typed mappers and never promise that every wearable exposes every record. Do not assume Health Connect supplies raw PPG/ECG waveforms.

## Product and privacy obligations

- [Google Play Health apps declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291): published/testing apps must accurately declare health features and data access.
- [India Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/02/Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Act-2023.pdf): consent withdrawal and cessation of processing inform pause/delete design.
- [OWASP MASVS](https://mas.owasp.org/MASVS/): storage, cryptography, authentication, network, platform and privacy controls for the Android security verification plan.

Decision: data minimization, purpose-specific permission, authenticated server identity, encrypted storage/transport, retention limits, export/delete and no health payload logging are release gates.

## Medical-device boundary

- [CDSCO Medical Devices Rules, 2017](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2022/m_device/mdr%2C%202017%20%281%29.pdf) and [draft Indian medical-device software guidance](https://cdsco.gov.in/opencms/resources/UploadCDSCOWeb/2018/UploadPublic_NoticesFiles/Draft%20guidance%20document%20on%20Medical%20Device%20Software%2021%2010%202025.pdf) show why intended use and claims determine a separate regulated path.
- [FDA Clinical Decision Support guidance](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) is a useful international reference for separating transparent information support from patient/caregiver device functions.

Decision: MVP output is research/wellness evidence, not diagnosis, treatment or guaranteed emergency detection. A regulated intended use requires a separate quality, validation and regulatory program.

## Signal and camera evidence

- [Motion artifact reduction for wrist PPG](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6387309/) supports using motion context and multichannel/raw-signal methods only when such sampled data exists.
- [Evaluation of biases in rPPG methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8175478/) reports real-world lighting/motion failure modes and demographic evaluation needs.
- [Demographic bias in public rPPG datasets](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12491395/) reports underrepresentation of darker skin tones in commonly used datasets.

Decision: camera MVP is capture-quality guidance. Any rPPG estimator stays behind a research flag until reference-sensor, device, motion, lighting and subgroup validation is complete.

## Authoritative real-signal validation

- [PPG-DaLiA](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/495/ppg%2Bdalia) provides 15 participants, wrist BVP at 64 Hz, acceleration at 32 Hz and ECG-derived HR ground truth. Its official readme specifies aligned 8-second windows with a 2-second stride.
- [BIDMC PPG and Respiration](https://physionet.org/content/bidmc/1.0.0/) provides 53 eight-minute critical-care recordings with 125 Hz PPG/ECG/respiration, 1 Hz HR/RR/SpO2 and manual breath annotations.
- [RRest](https://peterhcharlton.github.io/RRest/) documents respiratory-induced baseline, amplitude and frequency variations in PPG and transparent fusion research methods.
- [CapnoBase](https://doi.org/10.5683/SP2/NLB8IT) provides 42 benchmark-only PPG/capnography cases for an untuned external respiratory-rate test.
- [PhysioNet PTT-PPG](https://physionet.org/content/pulse-transit-time-ppg/1.1.0/) provides real synchronized paired optical channels from distal and proximal sensors for signal-path validation. Its README conflicts on red/infrared ordering, and its boundary spot SpO2 labels are insufficient for calibration.
- [Sleep-EDF Expanded](https://physionet.org/content/sleep-edfx/1.0.0/) provides polysomnography and expert hypnograms for sleep-session adapter validation.
- [scikit-learn GroupShuffleSplit](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.GroupShuffleSplit.html) documents group-aware splitting; Member 2 uses the participant as the indivisible group to prevent identity leakage.

Decision: PPG-DaLiA is the primary motion-aware benchmark, BIDMC is an explicitly separate external-domain sanity check, CapnoBase is a frozen respiratory benchmark, PTT-PPG validates dual-wavelength signal plumbing without fitting SpO2, and Sleep-EDF validates expert-annotation conversion. An engineering quality target is not a disease label. See the dataset register and predefined test protocol for licences, limitations and exact reporting.
